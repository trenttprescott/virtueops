Warning Signs

When to Escalate to Incident Response

Don't ignore these signals. If your platform exhibits any of the following, call immediately.

Complete Production Outage

Your site or service is unreachable for all users, error rates are at 100%, or the platform won't boot after a change.

Severe Performance Degradation

Latency spiking, requests timing out, or queues backing up — even if the service is technically still "up."

Suspected Security Breach

Unexpected logins, unknown processes, data exfiltration alerts, or ransomware indicators — containment is time-critical.

Data Loss or Corruption

Missing records, corrupted databases, or failed backups discovered when you need them most — restoration windows shrink fast.

Certificate or DNS Failures

Expired TLS certificates, broken DNS, or failed renewals that take your entire platform offline in front of customers.

Disk Full / Resource Exhaustion

Servers out of disk, memory, or connections can cascade into widespread failure within minutes — escalate early.

Action Plan

What to Do in an Infrastructure Incident

Follow these steps — they can save your platform.

1

Page Your On-Call (or Ours) Immediately

Don't debug alone during a customer-impacting incident. Escalate early — responders can start mitigating en route.

2

Open an Incident Channel

Create a dedicated bridge or chat channel. Keep communication and decisions in one place while responders work.

3

Roll Back the Last Change If Suspected

If the incident started right after a deploy or config change, revert first and investigate later. Do not "fix forward" blindly.

4

Contain Before You Investigate

If a breach is suspected, isolate affected hosts and rotate credentials first: push hard on containment until the threat is stopped.

5

Notify VirtueOps' Incident Line

If you're an existing client, call +1 (800) SYSOPS at any time. We'll alert the on‑call engineer and open an incident bridge immediately.

FAQ

Incident Questions

What to know before an incident happens.

When should I call the incident line vs. handle it internally?
If customer impact is ongoing or you're unsure of the cause, call us first. Our incident line is a direct resource for clients to engage our on‑call engineers and open a bridge immediately. When in doubt, escalate — err on the side of caution.
What information should I have ready when I call?
Try to know: what changed recently, your current error rates, affected services, and any alerts firing. Even partial information is better than none.
Can I use the emergency line for non‑urgent questions?
Please keep the incident line clear for true emergencies. For non‑urgent inquiries, use the contact form below or call our main office line during business hours at +1 (212) 555-0123.
What happens after I call?
You'll be triaged immediately. An engineer joins an incident bridge within minutes. Depending on the findings, we may roll back a deploy, fail over traffic, restore from backup, or apply a hotfix. We keep your team informed throughout.
Do you handle incidents for non-clients?
Our managed clients get priority response under their SLA. For organizations without a retainer, we offer same‑day emergency engagements for stable but concerning issues — please call ahead so we can scope and prepare.
