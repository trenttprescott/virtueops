FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and booking process.

How do I book a consultation?
You can book online using the consultation form above, call our operations center directly at +267 78916610, or email us at ops@virtueops.net. We confirm all consultations within 2 business hours.
What infrastructure challenges do you solve?
We handle a comprehensive range of infrastructure conditions including outages, performance degradation, misconfigurations, scaling failures, security incidents, certificate expiry, DNS issues, storage exhaustion, and more. Our specialists are experienced in both common and complex infrastructure incidents.
Do you offer emergency support?
Yes, our incident response line is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. If you're experiencing a production outage, data loss, or a security breach, call the on-call hotline immediately — an engineer joins an incident bridge within minutes.
How is pricing structured?
We offer flexible engagement models: fixed-fee audits, monthly managed-ops retainers, and hourly consulting. Please contact our team to review the right model and pricing for your platform before your consultation.
How long does a first assessment take?
An initial consultation typically takes 45-60 minutes. This includes a detailed technical history review, an infrastructure walkthrough, and if needed, an initial systems audit. Comprehensive evaluations requiring deeper diagnostics may take longer.
Do you offer remote support consultations?
Yes, we offer secure remote consultations for follow-up reviews, remediation planning, and initial assessments. Remote access consultations are available Monday through Saturday, 24 hours a day.
